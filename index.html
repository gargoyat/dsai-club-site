Demystifying AI

Proudly operating under the Sci-Tech Council of IIT Bhilai[cite: 2], we are a community of builders. If you have ever wondered how Netflix recommends movies, how self-driving cars see the road, or how ChatGPT writes code—that is Data Science and AI. We are here to help you learn how to build those exact systems from scratch.

  • Machine Learning: Instead of writing strict rules for a computer, we teach it to learn by showing it massive amounts of data examples.
  • Data Analytics: Acting like a digital detective to take messy numbers and turn them into beautiful, predictive graphs.
  • Artificial Intelligence: The ultimate goal. Building smart systems that can reason, predict, and solve complex human problems.

What We Actually Do

We are highly active on campus, transforming theoretical knowledge into hands-on experiences through continuous events and community building[cite: 3].

Group Discussions

Weekly roundtables where members debate the ethics of AI, break down new research papers, and brainstorm project solutions.

Expert Talks

We invite industry leaders, researchers, and alumni to share cutting-edge insights on where the AI industry is heading.

Hackathons

Intense, coffee-fueled coding marathons where teams compete to build functioning AI applications in just 24-48 hours[cite: 3].

Meraz Fest Events

Hosting flagship technical challenges, AI showcases, and competitive tech events during IIT Bhilai's annual techno-cultural extravaganza[cite: 2].

Start Your Journey

Ready to build with us? We welcome first-years from all branches—absolutely no prior coding experience required. We will teach you everything. The onboarding process is simple:

  • Introductory Session: Attend our orientation to see the cool tech we build and understand our vision.
  • Member Forms: Fill out a quick form to tell us what excites you—whether it is making AI art, trading stocks, or building robots.
  • The Chat (Interview): Don't panic! This is just a casual conversation to see how curious you are and how you approach puzzles.
  • Welcome Aboard: Selected members join the community and are immediately placed into a team to start building!
