DSAI Club

The Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Community of IIT Bhilai.

What We Do

We are a community of students who build things with data. Whether it is training machine learning models, analyzing complex datasets, or discussing the latest breakthroughs in AI, we provide a space for beginners and experts alike to learn together.

Our Focus Areas

  • Machine Learning: Building algorithms that learn from data.
  • Data Analytics: Finding patterns and stories in raw numbers.
  • Workshops & Hackathons: Hands-on sessions to turn theory into real projects.

Join the Club

Ready to build with us? We welcome first-years from all branches—no prior coding experience required. Drop by our next meetup or join our community channels to get started.
