DSAI Club

The Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Community of IIT Bhilai.

What We Do

Proudly operating under the Sci-Tech Council of IIT Bhilai, the DSAI Club explores the exciting fields of data science, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. We provide a collaborative platform for innovation where students develop practical skills and build intelligent, data-driven solutions.

  • Machine Learning: Building algorithms that learn from data.
  • Data Analytics: Finding patterns and stories in raw numbers.
  • Workshops & Hackathons: Hands-on sessions to turn theory into real projects.

How to Join as a Fresher

Ready to build with us? We welcome first-years from all branches—no prior coding experience required. The onboarding process follows these steps:

  • Introductory Session: Attend our orientation to learn about the club's vision and ongoing projects.
  • Member Forms: Fill out the recruitment application to tell us about your interests.
  • Interviews: A casual conversation to gauge your curiosity and passion for AI.
  • Announcement: Final members are selected and officially welcomed into the community!

Our Community

Our club thrives on a structured two-tier system to ensure everyone gets the guidance they need to succeed.

Core Members

Experienced students guiding projects, setting direction, and mentoring the incoming batch.

1st Year Members

Our newest innovators bringing fresh ideas, learning by doing, and building their portfolios.

Our Projects

We believe in learning by doing, not just watching. Every member contributes to meaningful, tangible work. Our project cycles typically run for 8-12 weeks, requiring just 2-3 hours of commitment per week to ensure a balance with academics.

From algorithmic trading to deep learning models, our code lives on GitHub. Explore our popular repositories, contribute, and learn from our open-source code base!
