The Mission

Welcome to the Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Club at IIT Bhilai! We are a community dedicated to exploring machine learning, analytics, and intelligent systems. By building hands-on projects and collaborating on cutting-edge research, we help students transition from beginners to skilled innovators. Join us to turn raw data into meaningful technology.

Want a 2-minute crash course? Open the Terminal in the bottom right corner and type learn.

What We Actually Do

We believe in transforming theoretical knowledge into hands-on experiences through continuous campus events and community building.

Group Discussions

Interactive roundtable sessions where members break down new research papers, debate AI ethics, and brainstorm collaborative tech solutions.

Expert Talks

We regularly invite industry leaders, technical researchers, and accomplished alumni to share insights on the future of the AI industry.

Hackathons

Intense, high-energy coding marathons where competitive teams build functioning artificial intelligence applications in just 24 to 48 hours.

Meraz Fest Events

We proudly host the flagship algorithmic challenges, tech showcases, and competitive data events during IIT Bhilai's annual festival.

Start Your Journey

Ready to build with us? We welcome first-years from all branches—absolutely no prior coding experience required. We will teach you everything. The onboarding process is simple:

  • Introductory Session: Attend our orientation to see the cool tech we build and understand our vision.
  • Member Forms: Fill out a quick form to tell us what excites you—whether it is making AI art, trading stocks, or building robots.
  • The Chat (Interview): Don't panic! This is just a casual conversation to see how curious you are and how you approach puzzles.
  • Welcome Aboard: Selected members join the community and are immediately placed into a team to start building!

How We Operate

Nobody learns alone here. Our club uses a two-tier mentorship system to ensure that every single beginner gets the personal guidance they need to succeed.
