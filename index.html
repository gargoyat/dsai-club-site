The Mission

The DSAI Club at IIT Bhilai explores the world of data science and AI through hands-on projects, research, and innovation. It offers a collaborative space for students to engage with intelligent systems and data-driven solutions. Through workshops, research initiatives, and peer learning, members gain practical skills and a deeper understanding of emerging technologies. The club encourages curiosity, problem-solving, and creativity in applying AI to real-world challenges. We warmly welcome you to IIT Bhilai and look forward to being part of your journey into data and intelligence.

What We Actually Do

We are highly active on campus, transforming theoretical knowledge into hands-on experiences through continuous events and community building.

Group Discussions

Weekly roundtables where members debate the ethics of AI, break down new research papers, and brainstorm project solutions.

Expert Talks

We invite industry leaders, researchers, and alumni to share cutting-edge insights on where the AI industry is heading.

Hackathons

Intense, coffee-fueled coding marathons where teams compete to build functioning AI applications in just 24-48 hours.

Meraz Fest Events

Hosting flagship technical challenges, AI showcases, and competitive tech events during IIT Bhilai's annual techno-cultural extravaganza.

Start Your Journey

Ready to build with us? We welcome first-years from all branches—absolutely no prior coding experience required. We will teach you everything. The onboarding process is simple:

  • Introductory Session: Attend our orientation to see the cool tech we build and understand our vision.
  • Member Forms: Fill out a quick form to tell us what excites you—whether it is making AI art, trading stocks, or building robots.
  • The Chat (Interview): Don't panic! This is just a casual conversation to see how curious you are and how you approach puzzles.
  • Welcome Aboard: Selected members join the community and are immediately placed into a team to start building!

Learn By Building

We firmly believe in learning by doing, not just watching boring lectures. From your very first week, you will contribute to meaningful, tangible work.

Our project cycles typically run for 8-12 weeks, requiring just 2-3 hours of commitment per week so it never interferes with your exams. You will learn real industry tools like Python, GitHub, and machine learning libraries.

Check out our open-source code base to see the facial recognition software, trading bots, and data dashboards we have already built!
